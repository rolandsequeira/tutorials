AdBlock Guard Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026

AdBlock Guard is an app for Wix websites that detects visitors who use an ad blocker and asks them to allow ads. It is made by Coders Creation, a sole proprietorship based in India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, for the Wix site owners who install it and for the people who visit those sites.

In short: AdBlock Guard does not collect, store or sell personal data about site visitors. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking. We run no servers for the app: it runs in the visitor's browser, and its settings are stored by Wix.

1. Site visitors

When someone visits a Wix site that uses AdBlock Guard, a small script runs in their browser. The script:

  • Checks whether a test element is hidden. Ad blockers hide page elements that look like ads, so the script adds one and checks whether it stays visible. This happens entirely inside the browser.
  • Checks whether ad requests are blocked (if the site owner has left this check on). The browser sends one request to Google's ad server at pagead2.googlesyndication.com, without cookies, and the script only notes whether the request was blocked. Like any web request, it reveals the visitor's IP address and browser type to Google, whose handling of it is covered by Google's Privacy Policy. The request does not carry any information about the visitor or the page beyond what every web request carries.
  • Checks whether the browser is Brave, using the browser's own navigator.brave feature, so Brave users can be shown how to turn off Brave Shields for the site.
  • Remembers a dismissed message for the rest of the visit. If the visitor closes the message, the script saves abg-dismissed in the browser's session storage. This is not a cookie, is never sent anywhere, and the browser deletes it when the tab is closed.

The results of these checks are only used to decide whether to show the message. They are not sent to us, to the site owner or to anyone else, and we never receive any information about individual visitors.

If the visitor's browser blocks all scripts, the site shows the message with plain HTML and nothing runs at all.

2. Site owners (Wix users)

Settings

The settings you choose in the AdBlock Guard dashboard (mode, text, colors, excluded pages and so on) are saved to your Wix site through Wix's embedded-script feature and stored by Wix. We do not keep a copy.

Information Wix shares with us

As with any Wix app, Wix gives us certain information when you install the app, such as your site's URL, the app instance ID, which plan you are on and, depending on Wix's settings, the site owner's contact email. The app uses the plan inside your dashboard to decide which features to show. We use this information only to provide the app, to answer support requests and to handle billing questions, and we do not sell or share it.

Payments

Premium plans are sold and billed by Wix. We never see or store your card or bank details.

Support

If you email us, we keep your message and email address for as long as we need them to help you and to keep a record of the request, then delete them.

4. Legal basis (EU and UK visitors)

For site owners, we process the information Wix shares with us to perform our agreement with you (providing the app and support) and for our legitimate interest in running the app. For visitors, the detection checks serve the site owner's legitimate interest in keeping their site funded by ads, and they involve no tracking, profiling or stored personal data.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, to object to its use, or to complain to your data protection authority. In India, this includes your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Because we hold no data about site visitors, visitors with questions about a particular website should contact that website's owner. Site owners can contact us at the address below. Uninstalling the app removes its settings from your site.

6. Children

AdBlock Guard is a tool for website owners and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

7. Changes

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this policy and change the date at the top. For significant changes, we will also tell site owners through the app or by email.
